# SmartShop Customer Policies

## shipping

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days when available. Delivery times can vary for remote addresses, holidays, or carrier delays.

## return

Most products can be returned within 30 days if they are unused and in the original packaging. Damaged, missing, high-value, or disputed orders should be reviewed by human support before promising a refund or replacement.

## payment

SmartShop supports major cards, bank transfer, and supported digital wallets. Payment details should never be shared in chat. Requests involving credit cards, chargebacks, suspicious payment activity, or personal data must be escalated for human review.

## warranty

Warranty coverage depends on the product and brand. Ask for the product ID or order ID so support can verify exact coverage before making a commitment.
